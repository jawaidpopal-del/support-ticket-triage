id: policy-hours
title: Opening hours
updated: 2024-06-03
---

Central libraries open 09:00 to 20:00 Monday to Thursday, 09:00 to 17:00 Friday and
Saturday, and are closed Sunday.

Branches open 10:00 to 18:00 Monday to Saturday. Bookmobile schedules are published
per route and change seasonally.
