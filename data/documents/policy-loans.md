---
id: policy-loans
title: Lending policy (current)
updated: 2025-01-15
---

Standard loans run for **21 days** for all borrower classes, including student and
institutional members. One renewal is permitted if no other borrower has placed a hold.

Reference material and items in the local history collection do not circulate and may
be consulted on site only.

Overdue items accrue no fine. Borrowing is suspended once an item is 30 days overdue,
and resumes when the item is returned.
